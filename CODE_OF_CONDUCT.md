# Code of Conduct

We want Crawlora's repositories and community spaces to be welcoming and
useful for everyone. Treat people with respect, discuss ideas constructively,
and make room for different experience levels and perspectives.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory
language, unwanted sexual attention, and publishing another person's private
information. Keep feedback focused on the work and its impact.

This policy applies in Crawlora organization repositories, issue and pull
request discussions, and other spaces when someone is representing the
Crawlora community.

Report a conduct concern through [crawlora.net/contact](https://crawlora.net/contact).
Reports will be reviewed and handled as appropriate. Do not use this route for
security vulnerabilities; follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for private
security reporting.
